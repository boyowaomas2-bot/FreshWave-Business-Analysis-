# FreshWave-Business-Analysis-
**CUSTOMER ANALYTICS FOR A TELECOM COMPANY ON BUSINESS STRATEGIES FOR REVENUE GENERATION, CUSTOMER RETENTION AND MARKETING PLANS - **
  INSIGHTS AND PERSPECTIVES FOR THE CONCERNS OF FINANCE, RETENTION AND MARKETING TEAMS.**

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

**_INTRODUCTION, OVERVIEW AND PURPOSE OF THE BRIEFS_:**
The project was set to find ways to address the respective concerns of the Finance, Retention and Marketing Teams,
tailored towards company’s operational efficiency in terms of revenue growth prospects, and focusing on new strategic 
customer-oriented actions, including measures for continued patronage, and targeted service delivery plans to mitigate 
high customer turnover, to achieve a steady revenue generation level, sustained customer service efficiency, as well as
general business growth and overall profitability. 

**_STATEMENT OF PROBLEM_:**
The concerns of the three Teams were observed to be inter-related. All showing different perspectives towards the same
goal but requiring specific focus on their respective functions and operating efficiency, with the company’s customers 
at the center, for revenue prospects, customer retention and marketing strategies. The briefs/problems are as enumerated
below:

1. **_FINANCE TEAM_:**    T
     To know how much revenue a customer is likely to generate based on their profile and the services
     they signed for.
2.	**_RETENTION TEAM_:**
      To find a way to identify and flag customers that are likely to cancel the services they signed for,
      so they can be contacted proactively.
3.	**_MARKETING TEAM_:**
      To find a way for the segmentation and grouping of customers for targeted and meaningful campaigns, so
  	  they can stop sending the same generic promotions to everyone.

**_METHODOLOGY AND APPROACH_:**
     The approach considered handling the different scenarios as external briefs from different clients. Methodology includes:
        1. Data Collection: Historical company dataset provided and made available. 
        2. Data Cleaning: Removed blank spaces and null values, changed datatypes, encoded and scaled data, etc.
        3. Data Modeling: Split test/train sets, trained, fitted, tested and predicted models but no train/test split for K- Means
        4. Visualization Designs: Created charts, showing segmentation cluster groups and their relationships to reflect company's 
           customers grouping arrangement. 
        
    * The respective concerns were subjected to relevant and appropriate scientific analysis, with the same 
      customer information data as provided, for observations and predictions, from the different supervised and
      unsupervised machine learning models used, as applicable with the independent and dependent variables of the data.
      
    * The predictive outcomes were evaluated with relevant metrics to assess the reliability and consistency of the models, 
      applied on the data, to establish the correctness of the findings and insights, give useful recommendations for
      management decisions, towards meaningful, proactive and necessary measures to address the concerns, and achieve 
      the set goals for company’s improved performance and stability.

**_EVALUATION, INTERPRETATION AND FINDINGS_:**

1.	**_FINANCE TEAM_:**
      • The test prediction of revenue estimates a customer is likely to generate as requested, based on subscribed
  	    services as established, is as shown below for only the first and last 3 customers and applies to all…
        24.96, 24.99, 19.92 ……. 19.96, 44.92, 29.94.
        This implies and confirms more revenue contribution with increased number of customers and subscribed services.

     • The metrics show that the variance between the predicted Mean Average Error (MAE) and actual values is 79% (+ or -)
  	   which confirms there are no significant errors, with Mean Squared Error(MSE) value of 1.11% also confirming a relatively
  	   low error margin and the r2 score margin of 99.88% shows a high prediction level of monthly charges with a low 0.12%
  	   variance as indicative of a direct relationship or effect of monthly charges on more or increased subscription as a huge
  	   for a higher revenue generation estimate.

     • Based on current services indices, customer revenue is sustainable and predictable and depends on numerical strength of
  	   customers, proportional and direct relationship between customer numbers and available range of services. This gives an
  	   assurance of a strong going concern status of the company’s ability to continue in business in the foreseeable future
  	   with a good revenue generation capacity, performance and profitability.
  	
2.	**_RETENTION TEAM_:** 
      • 79.91% of customers accurately and correctly classified as likely to consider cancelling or not with 67.18% precision,
  	    and 47.18% of them likely to cancel while 52.82% remained unconfirmed to be certain. 

      • The result show difficulties of fully identifying likely customers for effective remedial actions. However, a compensatory
  	   error test of 55.43% confirms a moderate number of likely customers to avoid false alarm while the confusion matrix on
  	   customers likely to cancel, identified 950 as not cancelling, 176 correctly identified /predicted cancelled, with 197 not
  	   identified and 86 as wrongly identified to cancel. 

      • The predictive probability estimates of likely to cancel, derived the predictions, and generated with customer ID, though
  	   not a 100% guarantee, is also a useful way to identify the customers for the proposed targeted and meaningful actions.
  	
      • Business expansion, improved service delivery, efficient new customer capture plan, to increase numbers is necessary for
  	   improved revenue growth.

3.	**_MARKETING TEAM_:**
      • The observations of the 5 predicted cluster groups show how segmentation and grouping of the customers into 5 distinct and
  	    highly concentrated groups, based on observed common characteristics, specific and similar patterns of subscribed services
  	    and profile, showed differentiated customers with same shared values and similar attributes clustering together and relating
  	    better with themselves. The grouping can apply to gender, partners, dependents etc., so they can be easily contacted individually
  	    and even as a group with the targeted campaigns.

     • Each of the identified 5 cluster groups are observed to be relatively homogeneous, closely knit and will relate, stick together
  	    and respond to common offers and campaigns, thereby making the grouping impactful.

**_INSIGHTS, BUSINESS IMPACT AND RECOMMENDATIONS_:**

1.	**_FINANCE TEAM_:**
      • The Finance Team is to closely monitor payment options for full revenue collection process on current services indices and new. 
      • Current level of revenue is established to be sustainable and predictable.
      •	Increased level depends mainly on numerical strength of customers with the direct proportional relationship between customer
  	    numbers and available range of services. 
      •	This affirms the assurance of a strong going concern status of the company and its ability to continue in business in the
  	    foreseeable future with a good revenue generation capacity, performance and profitability.

2.	**_RETENTION_:** 

      •	The Retention Team is advised to ensure creativity and find different proactive ways to design a customer retention plan as a project.
      •	The action plan to be closely monitored and managed to reverse any subscription cancellation intention and trend. 
      •	New measures are required with initiatives, to facilitate correct, accurate and error free identification process for customers likely
  	    to cancel, to mitigate the exposure on loss of customers and maintain stability of customer patronage. 

3.	**_MARKETING TEAM_:** 
      •	The Marketing Team is advised to go ahead with the plan creating 5 groups, to implement the segmentation and grouping strategies.
      • The Team is to determine and decide suitable, meaningful and impactful targeted campaigns with promotional offers relevant to each
  	    group’s needs for increased patronage, with new and attractive products, for a rise in customer numbers. 
      • The goal to creative offers and campaigns to sustain the current level of patronage, introduce new products with innovations,
  	    and attract new customers with efficient service delivery for sustainable business growth.

**_CONCLUSION_**
It is clear from the analysis above that there is a strong connection between the concerns of each team and the overall operational
performance of the business. The inter-relatedness focuses on customers to determine required actions for the teams and available 
services provided and new product lines. 
      
The concern of the Finance Team rests firmly on the success of the Retention and Marketing Teams, and the recommended action plans. 
These include proactive retention measures to minimize loss of customers, the ability of the Marketing Team to introduce effective 
customer segmentation strategies, for target-based and group-oriented campaigns, improved customer service delivery efficiency, with more
customer numbers and patronage from the new branding ideas.
      
The goal of accomplishing these combined operating strategies proactively, is geared towards a new and higher revenue level, increased
customer numbers and higher capacity with a new range of services, to achieve new growth levels, operational efficiency and profitability.
